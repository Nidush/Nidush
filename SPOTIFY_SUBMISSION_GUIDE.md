## 🚀 Submission Steps

### 1. Access Spotify Dashboard
1. Go to https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard
2. Sign in with your Spotify account
3. Select your app "Nidush"

### 2. Configure App Settings
1. Click "Settings" in your app dashboard
2. Ensure all information is complete and accurate
3. Add Privacy Policy and Terms of Service URLs
4. Verify all Redirect URIs are correct

### 3. Prepare Documentation
1. **App Description**: Write a detailed description explaining:
   - What your app does
   - How it uses Spotify data
   - Target audience
   - Key features

2. **Screenshots**: Take screenshots showing:
   - Login screen
   - Spotify integration
   - Music playback controls
   - Settings/preferences

### 4. Submit for Review
1. Scroll to "App Review" section
2. Click "Request App Review"
3. Fill out all required information
4. Upload screenshots
5. Submit the application

## ⏱️ Review Timeline

- **Initial Review**: 1-2 weeks
- **Follow-up**: May take additional time if changes are requested
- **Approval**: Once approved, your app can be used by any Spotify user
