# Spotify App Review Essentials

## Required Public URLs

- Privacy Policy: `https://nidush.com/privacy-policy`
- Terms of Service: `https://nidush.com/terms-of-service`

## Required App Configuration

- App Name: `Nidush`
- Client ID: `4a9f8400a01545008b0addcfa7a6b9c3`
- Redirect URI: `nidush://spotify-auth`

## Required Review Materials

- Short app description explaining what Nidush does
- Short explanation of how Spotify is used in the app
- Screenshots showing Spotify login, integration, and playback usage

## Submission Steps

1. Open `https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard`
2. Select the app `Nidush`
3. In Settings, add the Privacy Policy URL and Terms of Service URL
4. Confirm the redirect URI is correct
5. Open the App Review section
6. Submit the app description, Spotify usage explanation, and screenshots

## Note

Make sure the public policy pages are live before submitting the review.
